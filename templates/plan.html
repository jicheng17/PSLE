Phase 1 · Rebuild the foundations

Mon 28 Sep – Sun 4 Oct

One topic a day. For each: reread the topic on Science Sense, redo every "Your turn" question without peeking, then check your answers against the reveal.

Phase 2 · Strengthen and practise

Mon 5 Oct – Sun 11 Oct

Mixed practice by cluster, no more page-flipping while answering — check answers only after finishing each set. Midweek: one timed paper, then a full mistake review.

Phase 3 · Final countdown

Mon 12 Oct – Wed 14 Oct

Intensity tapers down as the exam nears — one more full paper, then lighter and lighter days.

Thursday, 15 October

Exam day

  • No new content this morning — a fresh brain beats a crammed one.
  • A quick 10-minute skim: just the labelled diagrams (flower, life cycles, water cycle, lungs, heart, circuits).
  • Eat a proper breakfast and leave early enough to arrive relaxed.
